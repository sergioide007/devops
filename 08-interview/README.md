## The 45-Minute Interview Structure

```
00:00 – 05:00  →  Introductions and background
05:00 – 15:00  →  Technical depth (your strongest area)
15:00 – 30:00  →  Scenario-based questions
30:00 – 40:00  →  Live exercise (write a script or design)
40:00 – 45:00  →  Your questions for the interviewer
```

---

## What American Companies Look For

1. **Technical depth** — not just "I know Kubernetes" but "here's how I solved X with Kubernetes"
2. **Communication** — can you explain complex things simply?
3. **Ownership** — did you build it, or just watch someone else?
4. **Metrics** — numbers speak louder than words (reduced deploy time 60%, reduced incidents 40%)
5. **Culture fit** — collaboration, learning, humility
