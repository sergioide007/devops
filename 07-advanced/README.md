## Why Advanced Topics Matter

In a 45-minute interview for a senior position, you will be asked:
- How do you manage 100+ microservices?
- How do you handle a major incident at 2 AM?
- How do you ensure security without slowing down deployments?
- How do you work with IoT or real-time systems?
- What is your experience with AI in DevOps workflows?

These topics show you are senior-level, not just someone who knows the tools.
